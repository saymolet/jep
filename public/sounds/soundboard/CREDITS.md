# Soundboard sound credits

The nine soundboard sounds (`<id>.mp3`, ids as in `app/utils/soundboard/messages.ts`) are
real recordings (or sampled real instruments, see notes) released under **CC0 1.0 Universal
(public-domain dedication)** — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . CC0
requires no attribution; the credits below are given as a courtesy and for provenance.

Changes made to every file: trimmed, short fade-in/fade-out, downmixed to mono, resampled to
44.1 kHz, peak-limited and loudness-normalized (EBU R128 two-pass `loudnorm`, target −16 LUFS,
true peak ≤ −1 dBTP), converted to MP3 (112 kbps CBR, mono). Every file is ≤ 3 s and < 41 KB.

## Notes

- **BigSoundBank** (Joseph SARDIN): each sound page states "License: CC0 (public domain):
  Free and royalty-free"; the licence page allows sharing, copying, distributing, adapting
  and commercial use without restrictions under CC0 1.0. Additional sounds: Joseph SARDIN –
  BigSoundBank.com.
- `drumroll` is described by its author as made with HALion Sonic SE in Cubase (sampled
  acoustic snare and cymbal).
- `tada` is described as made with Bravura Scoring Brass and Kontakt Factory Library
  (sampled real brass).
- `airhorn` is a stadium can air horn recorded with a Zoom H1 (the author added reverb/EQ).
- `laugh.mp3` is the soundboard's "Ha-ha" sound.
